Content Guide

Read before you arrive

Welcome to the MLB Global Crew. You made it through a very competitive selection and we are thrilled to have you.

Please read this day by day guide carefully before you arrive so we can work together smoothly on site.

Bring your own sense and mood and show the MLB Hip Pretty style in your own way.

On our side we have prepared plenty of MLB items and special events you can only experience on this project.

See you in Seoul!
Please note8 rules▾
  1. Keep logos from other brands out of your content. Fashion brands especially.
  2. Food and drinks may appear as small props only. Please do not make them the focus.
  3. Tag the MLB Hong Kong account in every post and story you upload during the collaboration.
  4. For street shooting keep other brand logos and signage out of the background as much as possible.
  5. MLB HQ and Hong Kong staff and our photographers will be with you on site. Tell us anytime if you need help and please follow their guidance.
  6. Let staff know if you need to wear or borrow extra items from the store for a shoot.
  7. Upload on the spot content as soon as you can. For content that needs editing please follow the deadline your contract will share later.
  8. Film at least 2 reels at each main location, such as the MLB stores.
