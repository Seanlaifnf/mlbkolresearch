Content Guide

Read before you arrive

Welcome to SEOUL, BY HAND — the opening of MLB AIRSIDE, Kai Tak.

Nothing on the day is watched; everything is made. You'll pick a pastel mocktail, bedazzle your own cup with your team's logo, finish a cupcake served in a mini cap, check your fit in the league mirror — and watch your own mark stitched live into the cap you wear out.

Bring your own sense and mood. Pastel, playful, K-fashion — and make it yours.

Shooting rules6 rules▾
  1. Only MLB in frame. Keep other brands' logos out — fashion brands especially.
  2. Here the cup and the cupcake are the content — shoot them freely. But drinks stay outside the store: park your cup in the caddy at the door before you step in.
  3. Hands first. Gems going on, cream piping, the needle running — close-ups travel further than a face.
  4. The concourse is public. Keep other shops' signage out of the background; your backdrops are the stand, the mirror, the storefront and the giant cap.
  5. MLB Hong Kong staff, Korea HQ and our photographers are on site. Ask anytime — and at the machine, follow the operator; hands stay clear of the needle.
  6. Want an extra piece from the store for a frame? Ask the staff.
Posting rules7 rules▾
  1. Tag @mlbkorea_hk_mc in every post and story.
  2. Add #MLBHongKong #MLBAirside #SeoulByHand #bedazzled to every feed post — carousels, reels, photos. Stories don't need them; use the AIRSIDE location sticker instead.
  3. Stories go up on the day — raw is fine, no editing needed.
  4. Carousel and reel within 3 days — by Sun 11 Oct. The proof post a week after the event — around 15 Oct.
  5. Your contracted piece is in your agreement with the agency. Everything else in this guide is a menu — extra pieces are always welcome.
  6. If your content is strong, MLB Hong Kong will invite you as a collaborator. No pressure — we'll accept the ones that fit best, and your post stays on your profile either way 😊
  7. Prefer not to be added as a collaborator? Please share the post to your story instead.
